Find any moment in a long video

Point it at security footage or a long recording. It skips the hours where nothing happens, works out who and what is in the rest, and lets you search it in plain words.

  1. Add a video. It stays where it is on your disk.
  2. Wait while it reads. Most of a recording is nothing happening, and that part is skipped.
  3. Search it. "a person carrying a box", or describe what you are looking for.
  4. Optional: name the people in it, and draw a line across a doorway. Then you can ask when did Rafi go out the front door.
